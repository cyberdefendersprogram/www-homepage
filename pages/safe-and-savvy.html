Safe and Savvy: Cyber Safety for Irvington Families

A parent’s playbook for the age of AI
WhenThursday, November 5, 2026, 6:00–7:00 pm PST WhereOnline on Zoom
View the slides Download PDF Download PDF with notes

AI can now clone a teen’s voice from a few seconds of video, fake a photo, or take over a friend’s account. In this interactive session, Vaibhav Bhandari (Security Engineer and Adjunct Faculty at Merritt College) shares practical, research-based steps for families. Topics include a family password, spotting scams (with tips from Fremont PD), privacy and location settings, group-chat bullying and AI-faked images, AI chatbots, password managers, and a shared family media agreement. Every parent leaves with one action to do tonight. A short bonus on careers in cybersecurity is available if there’s interest.

What we’ll cover

  1. Scams: is it really them?Voice clones, fake “police” calls, the family password, game-cheat malware
  2. Privacy and locationMeet Maya: what a stranger can piece together
  3. Group chats, bullying and appsWhat to say, which apps to know, three questions for any app
  4. AI: faked images and chatbotsWhat to do if it happens, and why a chatbot is a tool, not a friend
  5. Parenting: agree as a familyA family media agreement, one action for tonight, dinner-table questions

Every part starts with a question for the chat, so bring your own stories. Bonus side note: careers in cybersecurity. If there’s interest, we’ll do another seminar in more detail.

Your speaker

Vaibhav Bhandari is a Security Engineer and Adjunct Faculty at Merritt College. His background spans healthcare and cybersecurity. He has led software development at Optum and Microsoft, and is the author of an O’Reilly book. More at vitraag.com.
