What is AI Village?

AI Village is a hands-on way to learn how AI can be fooled, by trying it yourself in a safe game. Chatbots and AI assistants now sit in help desks, apps, email and school tools. Companies that build them need people who know how they break, and testing an AI by attacking it is a real job called AI red teaming.

The big idea has a name: prompt injection, the number one risk on the OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications. It happens when an AI follows instructions from the wrong person, because it can't always tell instructions from information. You don't need to code to try it. You just need words. It's a great first hack for someone new to security.

The name and the hands-on format come from the AI Village community and their open workshops. Our versions are small, student-run and built to fit a few minutes at an event.
