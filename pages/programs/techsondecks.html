Captain's Code

Skipper is the ship's computer. It knows a secret launch code and has been told not to share it. Your job is to change its mind. You don't need to hack anything or write code. You just need to be clever with words. When you think you have the code, type it in. Fewer messages means a higher score, and the top cadets show up on the live leaderboard at our table.

  1. Pick a callsign. A nickname, not your real name.
  2. Chat with Skipper. Ask, trick, persuade, role-play. Be creative.
  3. Submit the code. Clear a level to unlock the next, harder one.
  4. Save your badge. Download a Techs on Decks badge with your callsign and score.

What you're really learning

Chatbots and AI assistants are showing up everywhere, in help desks, apps and email. What you'll try at the table is called prompt injection, the number one risk on the OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications. Every level gets a stronger defense, so you will see first-hand why guarding an AI is hard, and why companies hire people to test them. That job is called AI red teaming.
